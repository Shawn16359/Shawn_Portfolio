RCM Denial Triage & Automation Agent : Work In Progress

Built a Revenue Cycle Denial Triage Agent to help guide denial resolution through structured workflows and decision logic. The current version supports CO-16, CO-18, CO-22, CO-27/31, CO-29, CO-150, and CO-197 denials, providing denial-specific recommendations and operational impact insights. I designed the workflows based on real-world revenue cycle operations and common denial management practices, with a focus on reducing revenue leakage, improving consistency, and accelerating denial resolution. Each playbook is tied to key operational metrics such as First-Pass Appeal Yield, Timely Filing Write-Offs, and A/R Over 90 Days. This project reflects my growing interest in AI-enabled workflow automation and how technology can support more efficient and scalable revenue cycle operations.

Every triage pathway directly targets core financial performance indicators, safeguarding metrics like First-Pass Appeal Yield, Timely Filing Write-Offs, and A/R Over 90 Days.
